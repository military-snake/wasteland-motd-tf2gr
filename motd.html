THE WASTELAND

The Wasteland is designed to provide an environment as close to the originally-intended gameplay as possible. Obviously, it's not Xbox 360 or PS3 levels of original, but it is as close as Gold Rush can provide as a platform. In doing so, I hope to provide a core TF2 experience very few people these days ever had the chance to receive.

The standard maps have been culled to remove anything that distracts from the arid landscapes of the original art style. Not all maps released post-launch fit the original art style quite perfectly, but they do the job well enough for the time being. The art style consists of four primary environments:

  • Desert — Dustbowl, 2Fort, Gravel Pit, Hydro
  • Farmland — 2Fort, Granary
  • Industrial — Hydro, Well
  • Mining — Dustbowl, Gravel Pit

These are the environments favored above all else in the map pool. This means no alpine (Lumberyard, Watchtower), no pure concrete (Turbine, Nucleus), and very few (if any) custom maps, as the intended experience demands a high level of curation and consideration before implementation. If a superior artpass of a standard map is brought to my attention, I may simply replace the standard map with the better-looking one for further cohesion.

The same principle applies to weapons, cosmetics, and potential server events.

  • No cosmetics allowed, end of story.
  • No unlocks allowed — stock weapons only, end of story.
  • No Halloween, Christmas, spring, or summer themes.

The Wasteland experience is designed to be consistent, stable, and internally cohesive. I don't want the diegetic tone of the server sacrificed for even a second, regardless of external events.

For general server functions, map timers are 45 minutes long across the board. Map nominations and Rock the Vote are enabled so players can consistently play on maps they prefer.

Construction note: not all features are implemented yet and some are still being tested. If features like stock-only enforcement or map nominations aren't working, they're being worked on.
